LOCKED  RULE   The  delivery  date  auto-fills  the  moment  status  becomes  Delivered.  No  manual  date  entry,  which  removes  a  whole  class  of  human  error. 
 
LOCKED  RULE   Sales  profit  before  ads  is  shown  per  order:  sales  price  minus  COGS  minus  delivery  fee.  This  is  the  profit  figure  before  advertising  is  considered. 
 
LOCKED  RULE   Role  scope:  a  CRS  sees  only  their  own  orders;  a  manager  sees  their  team's;  an  admin  sees  everything  including  money. 
 
LOCKED  RULE   Every  order  carries  its  funnel/offer  tag  so  the  Performance  section  can  isolate  advertising  ROI  per  offer. 
 
LOCKED  RULE   A  delivered  order  flows  the  customer  automatically  into  the  CRM  customer  database.  No  manual  customer  upload,  ever. 
 
LOCKED  RULE   Every  order  carries  a  'who  received  the  money'  flag  —  agent  (collected  COD  on  our  behalf),  company  account  (customer  paid  us  directly),  or  prepaid.  This  flag  is  set  at  entry  or  delivery  and  decides  whether  the  order  creates  an  agent  remittance  line  when  it  is  marked  delivered.  It  is  the  switch  that  routes  an  order  into  Wallet  or  not. 
Confirmed  decisions 
CONFIRMED  DECISION   Mark  delivered  fires  a  WhatsApp  update  to  the  customer  —  but  this  is  a  Q3  capability.  For  launch,  marking  delivered  sets  status,  auto-fills  the  date,  and  drops  inventory;  the  WhatsApp  fire  is  added  when  the  integration  lands. 
 
CONFIRMED  DECISION   The  person  who  adds  an  upsell  in  the  order  tab  is  the  CRS  who  gets  the  upsell-bonus  credit.  The  in-order  upsell  feeds  both  the  order's  revenue  and  that  CRS's  monthly  upsell  bonus  in  payroll. 
 
CONFIRMED  DECISION   Status  pipeline  is  new  →  confirmed  →  packed  →  dispatched  →  delivered,  with  failed,  returned,  and  lost  as  terminal  branches.  This  merges  the  storefront's  simpler  pipeline  with  the  COD  reality. 
Tiny  parts 
TINY  PART   Editing  an  already-delivered  order  changes  what  was  edited,  and  it  flows  through.  Fix  the  sales  price  on  a  delivered  order  and  the  profit  math,  the  dashboard,  and  the  analytics  all  update  to  match.  A  delivered  order  is  not  frozen  —  the  data  stays  truthful  to  the  correction. 
 
TINY  PART   The  order  list  is  sectioned  by  month  with  a  month  header  (e.g.  'JULY').  When  a  new  month  begins,  a  new  section  appears  at  the  top.

TINY  PART   The  CRS  can  mark  a  failed  delivery  and  its  expense  directly  in  the  order  tab  —  the  expense  is  captured  at  the  same  moment,  not  in  a  separate  step.

Mark  damaged  —  reduces  that  agent's  stock  and  records  it  as  a  loss.  Reason  required. 12.  Mark  missing  —  same  as  damaged  but  tracked  separately.  Reason  required. 13. LAYER  4   Rules 
LOCKED  RULE   The  warehouse  is  an  agent.  It  holds  stock  like  any  other  agent;  it  just  does  not  deliver. 
 
LOCKED  RULE   A  delivery  deducts  from  the  delivering  agent's  stock,  not  a  central  pool.  This  ties  directly  to  the  Orders  'inventory  drops  on  delivered'  rule. 
 
LOCKED  RULE   A  waybill-in  adds  stock  to  the  receiving  agent,  and  the  waybill  cost  flows  to  expenses.

LOCKED  RULE   Damaged  and  missing  both  require  a  reason  and  are  tracked  separately  from  sold  stock. 
 
LOCKED  RULE   Cost  of  inventory  equals  every  agent's  holdings  multiplied  by  cost  price,  summed  across  all  agents. 
 
LOCKED  RULE   Stock  can  never  go  negative  —  you  cannot  transfer  or  adjust  more  than  an  agent  holds. 
 
LOCKED  RULE   Every  movement  is  dated  and  attributed  to  whoever  did  it.  The  movement  history  is  the  control  that  keeps  self-service  adjustment  honest. 
Confirmed  decisions 
CONFIRMED  DECISION   Transfers  are  usually  regional  (same  location),  but  can  go  cross-region  —  for  example  Lagos  to  Abuja  —  in  special  circumstances.  Cross-location  is  allowed,  not  blocked;  the  reason  note  carries  the  justification. 
 
CONFIRMED  DECISION   Movement  history  is  included:  a  full  per-agent  audit  trail  of  every  waybill-in,  delivery-out,  transfer,  and  adjustment,  dated  and  attributed. 
 
CONFIRMED  DECISION   Both  CRS  and  manager  can  manage  stock  numbers,  transfers,  and  adjustments,  and  both  receive  low-stock  updates.  This  is  self-service,  not  admin-only;  the  movement  log  provides  the  oversight. 
Tiny  parts 
TINY  PART   Damaged  and  missing  stock  does  NOT  auto-flow  to  expenses.  Marking  something  damaged  is  a  judgment  call,  and  auto-posting  it  as  a  loss  would  let  stock  adjustments  silently  move  the  profit  number.  Damage/loss  reduces  the  agent's  stock  and  appears  in  shrinkage  reporting,  but  only  becomes  an  expense  if  the  owner  records  it  deliberately.  This  keeps  the  profit  number  honest. 
 
TINY  PART   Transfers  and  adjustments  both  require  a  written  reason  before  they  can  be  saved  —  the  same  discipline  applies  to  moving  stock  and  to  writing  it  off.

LAYER  4   Rules 
LOCKED  RULE   Every  profit  metric  uses  delivered  orders,  not  generated  —  undelivered  orders  have  earned  nothing. 
 
LOCKED  RULE   The  dashboard  is  a  filterable  engine:  every  metric  recalculates  on  any  filter  change.  It  is  never  a  static  view. 
 
LOCKED  RULE   ROAS  and  Business  ROI  are  never  merged.  They  are  always  two  separate,  clearly  labelled  numbers. 
 
LOCKED  RULE   Colour  tonation  follows  health  against  the  owner's  targets:  good  is  green,  mid  is  amber,  critical  is  red.  The  same  thresholds  drive  both  the  colour  and  the  red-flag  alerts. 
 
LOCKED  RULE   Real  CPA  uses  delivered  orders;  Platform  CPA  uses  generated  orders.  The  gap  between  them  is  the  hidden  cost  the  ad  platform  does  not  show. 
 
LOCKED  RULE   All  numbers  pull  automatically  from  orders,  inventory,  expenses,  and  ad  spend.  There  is  no  manual  dashboard  data  entry. 
 
LOCKED  RULE   Role-scoped:  a  CRS  sees  their  own  numbers,  a  manager  sees  the  team's,  an  admin  sees  all  money. 
 
LOCKED  RULE   Alerts  fire  to  the  notification  bell  now;  WhatsApp  and  email  delivery  come  in  Q3.

Tiny  parts 
TINY  PART   Ad  spend  on  failed  orders  is  an  ALLOCATION,  not  a  trace.  Ad  platforms  do  not  tell  you  which  specific  order  a  given  naira  of  spend  bought,  so  this  number  is  estimated:  total  funnel  spend  is  spread  evenly  across  the  orders  it  generated  (this  is  the  Platform  CPA),  and  the  failed  orders  each  carry  that  average  share. TINY  PART   The  allocation  assumes  failed  orders  cost  the  same  on  average  to  acquire  as  delivered  ones.  When  a  funnel  genuinely  attracts  worse-quality  orders  that  fail  more  often,  this  slightly  understates  the  true  loss.  The  assumption  is  noted  so  the  number  is  never  treated  as  gospel. 
 
TINY  PART   Platform  vs  Real  CPA  is  filterable  by  ad  platform,  so  Meta,  TikTok,  and  Google  spend  and  their  resulting  CPAs  can  each  be  seen  separately.

Outstanding  equals  only  the  COD  still  standing  with  agents.  Earned  minus  outstanding  equals  cash  in  hand  —  the  numbers  reconcile  end  to  end. 
 
LOCKED  RULE   Filterable  by  time  frame,  sub-brand,  and  category,  like  every  other  view. 
Confirmed  decisions 
CONFIRMED  DECISION   Accounting  is  the  lighter  version  for  launch  —  a  profit-and-loss  and  cash  view,  not  a  double-entry  engine.  Zoho  Books  already  handles  the  formal  bookkeeping,  so  rebuilding  it  now  would  be  wasted  effort.  The  full  engine  is  a  future  consideration,  not  a  launch  build. 
 
CONFIRMED  DECISION   Accounting  shows  both  earned  profit  and  cash  position  together,  because  'am  I  profitable  and  do  I  actually  have  the  cash'  is  one  question  a  founder  asks  at  once.  The  cash  line  (collected  vs  outstanding)  is  drawn  from  Wallet,  so  the  two  views  never  contradict  each  other. 
Parked  for  later  —  handled  in  Zoho  Books  for  now These  are  recorded  here  so  the  decision  is  not  lost.  When  the  business  is  ready  to  bring  formal  bookkeeping  
in-house,

Tiny  parts 
TINY  PART   Accounting's  income  must  match  the  Dashboard's  exactly  —  both  use  delivered-order  revenue.  If  one  counted  generated  orders  while  the  other  counted  delivered,  the  same  product  would  show  two  different  revenue  numbers,  which  destroys  trust  in  every  figure.  There  is  one  revenue  truth. 
 
TINY  PART   For  a  COD  business,  earned  and  collected  are  different  moments. The  gap  between  them  is  uncollected  COD  sitting  with  agents  —  a  number  COD  founders  rarely  see  and  badly  need.

LOCKED  RULE   Expenses  is  the  money-out  hub.  Some  expenses  flow  in  automatically  (ad  spend,  contractor  pay,  waybill  fees,  failed-delivery  and  agent-covered  costs);  the  rest  are  entered  directly.  Every  automatic  feed  can  also  be  entered  manually  —  automatic  is  an  option,  not  a  requirement. 
 
LOCKED  RULE   Four  fixed  parent  categories  —  operational,  advertising,  marketing,  building  —  each  with  custom-editable  sub-categories  the  business  defines. 
 
LOCKED  RULE   No  double-counting.  Anything  already  deducted  in  a  per-order  profit  calculation  never  appears  as  an  expense  line.  Cost  of  goods  and  the  delivery  fee  on  a  successful  order  are  per-order  deductions  (in  the  order's  profit),  not  expenses.  Only  costs  with  no  order  to  absorb  them  become  expenses. 
 
LOCKED  RULE   Advertising  is  isolated  so  ROAS  (return  on  ad  spend  only)  never  merges  with  Business  ROI  (return  after  all  four  categories)  —  the  same  rule  as  the  Dashboard. 
 
LOCKED  RULE   Product/offer  attribution  requirement  varies  by  category:  for  Advertising  it  is  mandatory  (ad  spend  without  knowing  its  product  and  offer  is  useless  for  CPA  and  ROAS);  for  Marketing  it  should  be  present  (creative  was  made  for  a  specific  product);  for  Operational  and  Building  it  is  optional  (rent  and  salaries  are  general  overhead  that  cannot  be  split  across  products  meaningfully). 
 
LOCKED  RULE   Auto-fed  expenses  carry  their  attribution  —  brand,  product,  offer,  platform  inherited  from  the  source  —  and  it  is  always  editable. 
 
LOCKED  RULE   The  expense  report  gives  a  visual  read  of  spending  by  category  and  over  time,  filterable  by  every  attribution  key  and  time  frame. 
Tiny  parts 
TINY  PART   The  hub  principle  is  what  keeps  one  clean  profit  truth.  Because  ad  spend,  contractor  pay,  and  failed-delivery  costs  all  converge  here  with  their  attribution,  the  Dashboard,  Accounting,  and  Performance  all  read  the  same  categorised  money-out  —  there  is  no  second,  conflicting  expense  number  anywhere. 
 
TINY  PART   Attribution  varies  by  category  deliberately.  Forcing  rent  to  attribute  to  a  single  product  would  produce  meaningless  numbers;  not  forcing  ad  spend  to  attribute  would  break  CPA  and  ROAS.  The  requirement  is  strict  exactly  where  it  matters  (Advertising)  and  relaxed  where  it  does  not  (Operational).

Tiny  parts 
TINY  PART   Ad  spend  flows  to  Expenses  carrying  its  full  attribution  (brand,  product,  offer,  platform,  and  the  Advertising  category),  so  an  expense  line  is  never  just  an  untagged  lump.  The  attribution  is  inherited  from  the  media-buyer  entry  but  remains  editable. 
 
TINY  PART   The  distinction  between  an  ad  as  a  name  and  an  ad  as  an  object  is  what  makes  the  catalog  valuable.  A  name  is  a  disposable  label;  an  object  accumulates  a  track  record.  Picking  the  object  in  the  weekly  review  is  what  lets  performance  build  up  per  creative  rather  than  being  lost  each  week. 
 
TINY  PART   Two  independent  attribution  reads,  neither  requiring  the  buyer  to  map  ad-IDs.  Path  one  is  the  weekly  review  —  the  buyer's  reported  view  from  their  ads  manager  (human  judgment).  Path  two  is  server-side  attribution  —  the  system's  own  measured  capture:  when  a  customer  orders,  the  server  records  where  they  came  from  (the  click-ID,  UTM,  and  source  data  carried  into  the  order).  This  measured  view  lives  in  the  CRM,  where  customers  can  be  filtered  by  delivered-versus-not  and  by  source.  The  two  coexist:  one  is  what  the  buyer  reports,  the  other  is  what  the  system  measures  directly.  The  catalog  link  between  a  platform  ad  and  a  specific  creative  is  deliberately  NOT  built  by  manual  ad-ID  mapping  (too  much  work  for  the  buyer);  creative-level  certainty  beyond  the  buyer's  report  is  future  work. 
 
TINY  PART   How  this  attribution  differs  from  dedicated  platforms  (Triple  Whale,  Northbeam,  Hyros):  Numbatrak  does  last-click,  first-party,  single-touch  attribution  anchored  to  delivered  orders  —  it  records  the  entry  point  of  every  real  order.  Those  platforms  do  multi-touch  journey  modeling,  cross-platform  deduplication,  and  view-through  attribution,  which  are  heavier  and  statistical.  For  a  COD  business  anchored  on  delivered  orders,  last-click  first-party  is  arguably  more  trustworthy  than  platform-reported  conversions  (which  are  inflated  and  deduplicated  by  guesswork).  Multi-touch  modeling  is  future  work  —  either  an  integration  with  one  of  those  platforms  or  a  Numbatrak  engine  built  later.

LOCKED  RULE   A  refund  reverses  the  sale  —  the  order  flips  to  Refunded  and  its  revenue  and  profit  come  off  the  books. LOCKED  RULE   A  replacement  books  a  loss  —  one  unit  out  of  inventory  plus  its  cost  plus  the  redelivery  fee,  with  no  new  revenue.  Both  refund  and  replacement  losses  land  in  a  Chargebacks  sub-category  under  Operational,  so  all  such  losses  are  traceable  in  one  place. 
 
LOCKED  RULE   Marketing  campaigns  consume  credits  from  two  separate  pools  (email,  WhatsApp).  Transactional  messages  are  included  in  the  subscription  and  do  not  consume  credits.
