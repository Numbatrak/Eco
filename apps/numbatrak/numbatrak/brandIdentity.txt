Brand Identity Guidelines

Know Your Numbers.
This document defines every visual and verbal decision behind the Numbatrak brand. It is the single source of truth for any designer, developer, marketer, or partner who represents this product publicly.

— SECTION 01 —
What Numbatrak Is


Numbatrak is a performance intelligence platform. It tracks the numbers behind business growth — revenue, ad spend, ROAS, campaign results, and the metrics that tell operators whether their growth is real or just activity.

It was built to solve a specific problem: founders and operators running paid media across multiple campaigns, products, and markets have no single place where all their performance data makes sense together. Numbatrak is that place.

The One-Line Truth
"Numbatrak makes the numbers that matter impossible to ignore."
What Numbatrak Is
A performance intelligence platform for growth operators
A SaaS tool that tracks paid media performance across campaigns and brands
A clarity layer between raw ad data and business decisions
A global product — built for operators anywhere, not one market
A standalone brand that speaks entirely for itself

What Numbatrak Is Not
An agency tool or white-label dashboard
An African-only product or regional brand
A financial services platform or payment processor
A lifestyle or emotional brand
A tool for casual users — it is built for serious operators

Who Uses Numbatrak
Numbatrak is built for performance operators — the founders, media buyers, growth leads, and marketing directors who live inside paid media data and need to see it clearly, fast, and in one place.

E-commerce founders running multiple product lines and campaigns
Media buyers managing ad spend across several brands or clients
Growth leads at funded startups who report performance to stakeholders
Marketing directors who need clean performance visibility without analyst overhead

Emotional Territory
When someone opens Numbatrak, they should feel five things:

Precision — every number is exactly where it should be
Control — I can see everything that matters from one screen
Clarity — no noise, no confusion, no guessing
Confidence — I know what's working and what isn't
Intelligence — this tool thinks the way I think

— SECTION 02 —
Brand Strategy


Positioning Statement
"Numbatrak is a performance intelligence platform for growth operators. It turns the noise of paid media data into clear, actionable visibility — so founders and operators always know exactly where their growth comes from."

Brand Tagline

Know Your Numbers.

Three words that communicate everything the product does. It is not aspirational — it is direct. It is an instruction and a promise simultaneously. Every founder who has ever felt lost in their ad data understands immediately what this means.

TAGLINE USAGE RULES
Always used in full: 'Know Your Numbers.' — including the period
Never abbreviated, reworded, or split across lines in logo lockups
Appears in Emerald (#10B981) on dark backgrounds
Appears in Midnight (#0F172A) on light backgrounds
Never appears in gray, white, or any color that reduces its authority

Brand Personality
Numbatrak should feel like the smartest tool in the room — not the loudest. It does not need to shout. It earns trust through precision, consistency, and the feeling that everything is under control.

FIVE PERSONALITY TRAITS
every number, every label, every data point is exactly right Precise —
nothing exists in the interface or the brand that does not serve a purpose Minimal —
the product and the brand reflect structured, clear thinking Intelligent —
understated confidence, not aggressive marketing Confident —
speaks to operators anywhere, not positioned as a regional product Global —

Brand Archetype
Numbatrak operates as The Sage — the brand that gives people knowledge, clarity, and the tools to understand their situation accurately. Secondary archetype: The Ruler — structured, in control, everything in its place.
The Sage earns loyalty not through emotion but through consistent accuracy. Every time Numbatrak shows a user their numbers clearly, it builds the brand.

Midnight  #0F172A   +   Emerald  #10B981

— SECTION 04 —
Logo System


Logo Architecture
The Numbatrak logo consists of two elements used together or independently depending on context: the Symbol Mark and the Wordmark.

The Symbol Mark
Five ascending vertical bars representing tracked performance data — a bar chart rising from left to right. The mark is abstract enough to be ownable as a standalone icon, clear enough that its meaning (tracking, performance, growth) is immediately understood.

SYMBOL MARK CONSTRUCTION
Five bars of ascending height: approximately 40%, 65%, 50%, 100%, 80% — the tallest bar is the fourth, not the last, reflecting real data rather than a perfect ramp
Bar width to gap ratio: 4:3 — bars are slightly wider than the gaps between them
Corner radius on bars: 2px at standard size, scale proportionally at other sizes
The mark sits inside a rounded square container at 11-12px corner radius

SYMBOL MARK VERSIONS
Primary: Emerald gradient fill (#10B981 → #34D399) container, white bars — for use on Midnight and dark backgrounds
Outlined: Midnight container with Emerald border (1.5px), Emerald bars — for use on light backgrounds
Ghost: Deep Navy container with Emerald bars at 80% opacity — for subtle placements
Monochrome White: All white — for use on Emerald backgrounds or special contexts

The Wordmark
The Numbatrak wordmark is set in Inter ExtraBold (weight 900) with a deliberate split color treatment that makes the brand name visually distinctive and immediately memorable.

WORDMARK CONSTRUCTION
Typeface: Inter ExtraBold · Weight: 900
Letter spacing: -0.04em — tight, confident, modern
'Numba' — rendered in Emerald gradient (#10B981 → #34D399 left to right)
'trak' — rendered in Pure White (#FFFFFF)
The gradient flows naturally from the green portion into the white portion — no hard break
No italic, no outline, no shadow — clean and precise

WORDMARK SIZE HIERARCHY
Hero / Cover: 48-72px — full wordmark with tagline below
Standard: 28-40px — most common usage in marketing materials
UI / Navigation: 16-20px — product interface, app header
Minimum legible size: 14px — below this, use the symbol mark alone

Full Logo Lockup
The primary logo is the symbol mark + wordmark in a horizontal arrangement. The mark sits to the left of the wordmark, vertically centered. Below the wordmark, the tagline 'Know Your Numbers.' appears in Cool Gray at 45% of the wordmark's point size.

LOCKUP SPACING RULES
Gap between mark and wordmark: equal to the height of the mark
Tagline sits 8px below the wordmark baseline
Minimum clear space around the full lockup: equal to the height of the symbol mark on all sides
Never crowd the logo — it needs breathing room to carry authority

Logo File Specifications
Every designer working with Numbatrak must have access to the following file formats. Request these from the brand lead before beginning any design work.

REQUIRED FILE FORMATS
SVG — Primary format for all digital use. Scales infinitely without quality loss. Used for web, app, and screen-based materials
PNG (transparent background) — For use in documents, presentations, and contexts where SVG is not supported. Minimum export at 1000px wide at 2x resolution
PNG (dark background) — Pre-composed version on Midnight for quick use in social media and content
PNG (light background) — Pre-composed version on white/light gray for documents and print
PDF — For print applications. Vector-based, CMYK color profile
ICO / favicon — 16x16, 32x32, 64x64 — symbol mark only

COLOR PROFILES
Digital (screen): RGB color mode — use hex values as specified in this document
Print: CMYK conversion — Emerald (#10B981) converts to approximately C:78 M:0 Y:45 K:28
Single color print: All Midnight or all Emerald — never grayscale unless no other option

Logo Do's and Don'ts
✓  DO
Always use the approved logo files — never redraw, recreate, or approximate the logo


✓  DO
Maintain minimum clear space — equal to the symbol mark height on all four sides


✓  DO
Use only approved background colors: Midnight, Deep Navy, Pure White, or light gray


✗  DON'T
Stretch, compress, skew, or alter the proportions of the logo in any way


✗  DON'T
Add drop shadows, gradients, glows, or effects to the logo


✗  DON'T
Change the wordmark colors — 'Numba' is always gradient green, 'trak' is always white on dark


✗  DON'T
Place the logo on busy or photographic backgrounds without a clear zone or overlay


✗  DON'T
Use the wordmark below 14px — use the symbol mark alone at small sizes


✗  DON'T
Place the logo on any color outside the approved background colors


— SECTION 05 —
Typography


Typography is the voice of Numbatrak made visible. The system is deliberately minimal — two typefaces, a clear hierarchy, and rules that ensure everything reads with the same precision the product delivers.

Primary Typeface: Inter
Inter is the brand's display, headline, and wordmark typeface. Designed specifically for screen legibility, it is geometric, clean, and carries authority at every weight. Used for all headlines, the wordmark, stat numbers, navigation, and any text that leads.

DOWNLOAD: FONTS.GOOGLE.COM/SPECIMEN/INTER

Secondary Typeface: DM Sans
DM Sans is the body and supporting text typeface. Slightly more humanist than Inter, it provides warmth and readability in long-form content, descriptions, and captions without competing with headlines.

DOWNLOAD: FONTS.GOOGLE.COM/SPECIMEN/DM+SANS

Monospace Typeface: DM Mono
DM Mono is used exclusively for data, metrics, hex codes, technical values, and any content that is number or code-based. It signals precision and reinforces the data-first product identity.

DOWNLOAD: FONTS.GOOGLE.COM/SPECIMEN/DM+MONO

Typography Rules
Headlines always left-aligned in content layouts — center alignment only for full-width hero statements
All performance metrics and numbers use DM Mono — never Inter or DM Sans for numerical data
Minimum body text: 14px on screen — never smaller

Maximum line length for body copy: 680px or 75 characters — beyond this, readability drops
Never use ALL CAPS for body text — uppercase is reserved for labels and category tags at 10-12px
Letter spacing for uppercase labels: 0.10 to 0.15em

— SECTION 06 —
Imagery & Design Elements


Photography Philosophy
Numbatrak is a data product. Photography is not the primary visual language — data visualization, interface screenshots, and structured graphic compositions are. When photography appears, it must feel intentional, not decorative.

APPROVED PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE
Real operators at work — founders at desks, media buyers reviewing dashboards, growth leads in meetings. Always authentic, never staged-looking
Technology in context — screens showing real data, devices in real work environments, tools being used
Dark, moody environments that echo the Midnight brand palette — avoid bright, airy stock photography
Monochromatic or low-saturation images work best — they don't compete with the Emerald accent

PHOTOGRAPHY DON'TS
✗  DON'T
Use bright, saturated stock photography with people smiling at cameras — it contradicts the brand's precision positioning


✗  DON'T
Use abstract concept photography (lightbulbs, handshakes, growing plants) — Numbatrak communicates through data, not metaphor


✗  DON'T
Use photographs as backgrounds behind text without a dark overlay — always ensure text legibility


Data Visualization
Data visualization is Numbatrak's primary visual language. Charts, graphs, metric cards, and performance displays are the most authentic expression of what the brand is. These should appear in marketing materials, presentations, and social content.

VISUALIZATION STANDARDS
Primary chart color: Emerald (#10B981) for positive metrics, Cool Gray (#94A3B8) for neutral data
All chart backgrounds: Deep Navy (#1E293B) or Midnight (#0F172A)
Chart labels and axis text: Cool Gray (#94A3B8) in DM Mono
Highlighted data points: Soft Emerald (#34D399) with subtle glow effect
Never use red for negative metrics in marketing materials — Cool Gray communicates neutrality without alarm

Design Elements
Beyond photography and data visualization, Numbatrak uses a set of consistent graphic elements that can appear across all brand touchpoints.

APPROVED GRAPHIC ELEMENTS
Subtle grid or dot-matrix backgrounds on Midnight — used sparingly to add depth without clutter
Thin horizontal rules in Cool Gray at 1px — used to separate sections, never as decoration
Emerald glow/bloom effect — a soft radial gradient of Emerald at 6-8% opacity, used behind hero elements on dark backgrounds
Rounded rectangles for cards and containers: 12-16px corner radius consistently
Thin Emerald left border on blockquotes and highlighted callouts

ELEMENTS TO AVOID
Gradients across large background areas — reserved for the wordmark and logo mark only
Decorative illustration or iconography that is not geometric and precise
Drop shadows heavier than 0 4px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.3) — Numbatrak uses restraint
Animated elements that distract from data — motion should serve information, never decorate it

— SECTION 07 —
Brand Voice & Tone


How Numbatrak speaks is as important as how it looks. Every headline, tooltip, onboarding message, marketing copy, and social post is a brand moment. The voice must always feel like a precise, intelligent instrument — never a salesperson, never a cheerleader.

The Voice in Three Words

Precise.  Minimal.  Intelligent.
Writing Principles
Specificity over generality — 'tracks ROAS, revenue, and spend across 9 campaigns' beats 'tracks your performance'
Function over promise — describe what the product does, not what it will make you feel
Short sentences, active voice — 'Numbatrak shows you where your money goes' not 'Revenue visibility is provided'
Data language where possible — numbers, metrics, and specifics are more credible than adjectives
Neutral confidence — never hype, never shout, never oversell. State the truth clearly
Zero filler words — remove 'simply', 'easily', 'just', 'powerful', 'seamless', 'robust' from all copy

Tone by Context

PRODUCT UI / MICROCOPY
Ultra-concise. Every word earns its place. Labels are factual, not cute. Error messages are specific and actionable, not apologetic. Success states are quiet and clear.

✓  DO
'Revenue · Last 30 days' as a chart label — specific, clean


✗  DON'T
'Awesome! Your data is ready!' as a success state — never this


MARKETING & LANDING PAGE
Confident, benefit-led, data-grounded. Lead with what the product does for the user. Use real numbers where possible. CTAs are direct — never 'Learn More', always a specific action.

✓  DO
'See exactly where your growth comes from.' as a headline — precise, intriguing


✗  DON'T
'The ultimate performance tracking solution for modern businesses' — vague, forgettable


SOCIAL MEDIA
Educational and specific. Share insights, data observations, and framework thinking. Never motivational. Never generic. The content should make operators feel smarter for reading it.

✓  DO
'Most founders misread ROAS because they include brand campaigns in the average. Here is how to read it correctly.'


✗  DON'T
'Growth is a mindset. Track your numbers and scale what works!' — this is not Numbatrak's voice


ONBOARDING & SUPPORT
Patient and precise. Assume the user is intelligent. Give them the information they need without over-explaining. When something goes wrong, be specific about what happened and how to fix it.

— SECTION 08 —
Brand Misuse


This section documents the most common ways the Numbatrak brand can be misrepresented. Every person who produces Numbatrak materials must review this section. Consistency is the brand — any deviation weakens it.

Logo Misuse
✗  DON'T
Stretching or compressing the logo mark or wordmark disproportionately


✗  DON'T
Applying a drop shadow, outer glow, bevel, or emboss effect to the logo


✗  DON'T
Placing the logo on a background color that is not Midnight, Deep Navy, Pure White, or light gray


✗  DON'T
Recreating the logo from memory or approximating it in Canva or Figma without the official files


✗  DON'T
Changing 'trak' from white to any other color — it is always white on dark backgrounds


✗  DON'T
Using the wordmark at sizes below 14px — use the symbol mark alone


Color Misuse
✗  DON'T
Using Emerald as a large background color across hero sections or full pages


✗  DON'T
Introducing any color not in the 6-color system — purple, orange, red, yellow are never Numbatrak colors


✗  DON'T
Using Cool Gray (#94A3B8) for headlines, CTAs, or any primary content


✗  DON'T
Applying the Emerald gradient to elements other than the wordmark and logo mark


Typography Misuse
✗  DON'T
Using any typeface other than Inter, DM Sans, or DM Mono in Numbatrak materials


✗  DON'T
Using display or decorative typefaces — even temporarily for social posts


✗  DON'T
Using Inter or DM Sans for performance metrics — always DM Mono for numbers


✗  DON'T
Setting body copy tighter than 1.5 line height — readability is a brand value


Voice Misuse
✗  DON'T
Using hype language: 'game-changing', 'revolutionary', 'world-class', 'powerful', 'seamless'


✗  DON'T
Motivational or inspirational copy that does not reference product function or real data


✗  DON'T
Passive voice constructions — Numbatrak always speaks actively and directly


✗  DON'T
Exclamation marks in product UI or marketing headlines — confidence does not need punctuation to shout

Data slides: Large DM Mono number in Emerald, DM Sans explanation below in white

Email
Transactional email: White background, Midnight headlines, Emerald CTA buttons
Marketing email: Same as above — Numbatrak never sends dark-mode email as the default format
Email signature: Wordmark only (no full lockup), 'Know Your Numbers.' in Emerald below name
No animated GIFs, no heavy imagery — performance data visuals only where relevant

App Icon
Mobile app icon: Symbol mark centered on Midnight (#0F172A) background, Emerald gradient bars
Favicon: Symbol mark on Midnight at 32x32 and 16x16 — bars simplified to 3 at 16px
No wordmark in icon treatments — the mark must work completely alone at small sizes
